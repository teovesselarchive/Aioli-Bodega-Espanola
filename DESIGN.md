# Aioli: linger at the table

Mode: Persuade. A print-inspired restaurant identity with large Bodoni Moda lettering, pragmatic DM Sans utility type, plum ink (#342336), pale paper (#f7f4ed), and citron (#dbe78e) for actions and selection. Original red Aioli logo remains intact. Design variance 8, motion 3, density 3. Custom native CSS, no framework or design-system claim.

First viewport: a confident two-line invitation beside a large original paella photograph; generous type on paper, no text obscuring food. An offset wine-shelf inset establishes the bodega. Distinct from Café X’s full-bleed photographic poster. Subsequent rhythm: concise introduction, uneven food-photo spread, working menu preview, an intimate interior-photo composition, visiting information.

Quality reference: the approved Café X project and the user’s latest standing preferences. Reuse audit, outside research, original client assets, complete implementation, targeted mobile composition and honest behavior. No decorative arrows, X marks, theme switch, copied slogans, forced cultural copy, mock bookings or fabricated testimonials.
