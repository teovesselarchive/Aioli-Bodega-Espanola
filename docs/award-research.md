# Aioli Bodega Española — second-pass restaurant design research

Research and visual inspection: 4 November 2026. Scope: six additional restaurant references beyond 7 Portes, El Amigo and Jupiter. Exact distinctions below were verified on the primary CSS Design Awards pages. Awarded archive screenshots are distinguished from current live sites. Visual descriptions are direct observations, and the proposed Aioli applications are design inferences.

## Strongest principles for the redesign

Aioli needs a coherent bodega identity, not more miscellaneous effects. The most useful combination here is Genesis' singular graphic world, Völlerei's culinary objects as composition, Locanda Margon's unusual food-photo arrangement, and Burrito Madre's immediately usable food navigation. A poster-like typographic system with an oxblood/parchment base and deliberate cobalt controls can be original to Aioli; none of these references justifies copying its exact layout or visual marks.

Preserve Aioli's own garlic/olive logo, actual dishes, chandelier, ceramic pitcher and wine shelves. Those objects can supply specificity that another oversized serif sentence cannot. Treat the menu as a legible editorial document with strong category hierarchy, persistent category navigation and actual searchable text. Price, description and wine bottle/glass distinctions must remain clear.

## 1. Genesis Restaurant — a complete graphic world

- **Verified distinction:** CSS Design Awards Website of the Day, **17 September 2018**.
- [Primary award record](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/genesis-restaurant/33655/)
- [Awarded screenshot](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2018/201809/20180907170523.jpg)
- [Official URL linked by award](https://eatgenesis.com/) — archived design was inspected; current live behavior was not claimed or tested.
- Design credit: Herdl, collaborator Samuel Day.

**Observed:** stark black and white hand-drawn scene, an irregular stone-like GENESIS wordmark, human figures, serpent, sun, moon, plants and a burger in a radiating medallion. Illustration occupies the whole composition; the type looks like part of that world rather than a generic headline pasted above a card grid.

**Useful for Aioli:** establish one unmistakable art direction using its own culinary and architectural cues. Display lettering can have body and irregularity while information typography remains calm. A modern Spanish tavern poster is a better creative target than anonymous fine-dining editorial.

**Do not copy:** the biblical imagery, enter screen, woodcut illustration, exact lettering or graphic density. The archive is a graphic principle reference, not evidence of accessible modern menu interactions.

## 2. VÖLLEREI — real culinary objects carry the personality

- **Verified distinction:** CSS Design Awards Website of the Day, **10 December 2017**.
- [Primary award record](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/v-llerei-restaurant-bar/31921/)
- [Awarded landing view](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2017/201711/20171128071858.jpg)
- [Awarded navigation/table view](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2017/201711/20171209212514.jpg)
- [Awarded seasonal-food section](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2017/201711/20171209212522.jpg)
- [Official URL linked by award](https://www.voellerei.at/en/)
- Design credit: dreist., Austria.

**Observed:** an isolated, textured chef's knife and rosemary sit in front of a very faint oversized V. Condensed bold headings and small serif supporting text create hierarchy. The seasonal section uses three irregularly arranged photographed plates; the negative space belongs to their real shapes. A separate dark navigation view centers a warm overhead table photo and shows slivers of neighboring chapters.

**Useful for Aioli:** crop and compose its real ceramic vessel, food, wine and chandelier details as distinctive objects and scenes. Use weight and scale changes instead of adding more decorative UI. Let round plates interrupt the rectangular rhythm selectively. Dark and light sections can shift the mood without a theme selector.

**Do not copy:** the giant letter background, carousel-only navigation, decorative arrows, pale gold accent or exact arrangement. Archived screenshots establish composition only; no live interaction claim is made.

## 3. Locanda Margon — photography can break the split-hero template

- **Verified distinction:** CSS Design Awards Website of the Day, **7 January 2019**.
- [Primary award record](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/locanda-margon-restaurant/34304/)
- [Awarded landing screenshot](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2018/201812/20181218104811.jpg)
- [Awarded menu entry screenshot](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2018/201812/20190107011304.jpg)
- [Additional archived screenshot](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2018/201812/20190107011703.jpg)
- [Official URL linked by award](https://www.locandamargon.it/)
- Design credit: Quamm, Italy.

**Observed:** the opening image is a tight human gesture—hands pulling out a chair—rather than the expected plated-dish hero. The menu entry arranges six food photographs at staggered heights around a central oversized title, producing a loose constellation. The confidence comes from composition and photography, not many interface ornaments.

**Useful for Aioli:** vary photographic scale and rhythm. Give the full food menu a memorable entrance with a controlled composition of its actual dishes, then switch to dense, readable text. Details of the dining experience can convey hospitality better than generic aspirational copy.

**Do not copy:** the exact six-photo layout, thin type or white-on-white luxury aesthetic. The 2019 award record's Michelin statement is historical, not a verified current Michelin rating.

## 4. Burrito Madre — assertive identity and genuinely browsable food

- **Verified distinction:** CSS Design Awards Special Kudos, **30 June 2026**. This is **not** Website of the Day.
- [Primary award record](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/burrito-madre/49666/)
- [Awarded screenshot](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2026/202606/20260625155148.jpg)
- [Current live homepage](https://burritomadre.rs/en/)
- [Current live menu](https://burritomadre.rs/en/menu/)
- Design credit: General Condition, collaborator Futurianz.

**Observed archive:** dense illustrated ensemble of comic characters around a cream brand medallion on warm orange. The identity is unmistakable at a glance.

**Observed current live site:** warm cream, dark red-brown and punchy orange; very heavy, compact, slightly irregular display type. The homepage showed an enormous close-up of the product with oversized lettering in its video. The actual menu has a horizontal strip of labeled food-category cutouts, followed by large category headings and products. Clicking TACOS updated the URL to `#tacos` and scrolled the page. It is a browsable website, not a scan viewer. Individual item pages are linked.

**Useful for Aioli:** the transferable ideas are unmistakable type, a confident warm palette, real dishes at useful scale, and a category system that stays visually present. Aioli can use calmer text rows instead of a fast-food product grid and still retain this navigational clarity. Its menu can have a richer identity without sacrificing quick scanning.

**Do not copy:** comic characters, orange/green brand palette, fast-food grid, promotional badges or decorative arrow buttons. Current live design has changed from the archived illustration-led award image. The header also wraps awkwardly in the observed viewport; do not treat every award-site detail as good UX.

## 5. 1NUL8 — the room itself is a distinctive brand asset

- **Verified distinction:** CSS Design Awards Special Kudos, **25 April 2023**. This is **not** Website of the Day.
- [Primary award record](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/1nul8-restaurant/43372/)
- [Awarded screenshot](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2023/202304/20230419101157.jpg)
- [Current location page inspected](https://www.1nul8.nl/de-meent)
- Design credit: MR BOOST; current footer credits Code by Dennis.

**Observed archive:** broad interior image with graffiti-covered walls, plants and warm lights; a huge plain sans-serif location title sits along its bottom edge. A round crest overlaps a fine horizontal header rule. The website gets its character from a real room rather than arbitrary decoration.

**Observed live:** strong interior photography and repeated oversized location lettering; visible menu and reservation access. The menu content still links to lunch/dinner PDFs. A LIGHT/DARK control appears in the header.

**Useful for Aioli:** its actual ceramics, ironwork, wine and food should determine the website's texture. Small practical details—opening hours and reservation access—can coexist with visual drama.

**Do not copy:** theme switcher, repetitive marquee, crest layout or PDF-only menu. Aioli explicitly needs to improve on that last pattern with full text content.

## 6. Overseas — single-color identity anchored in illustration

- **Verified distinction:** CSS Design Awards Special Kudos, **16 February 2023**. This is **not** Website of the Day.
- [Primary award record](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/overseas/42932/)
- [Awarded screenshot](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/cdasites/2023/202302/20230211162141.jpg)
- [Official URL linked by award](https://www.overseasasianrestaurant.com/) — live visit failed with an SSL/cipher error; only the award archive was inspected.
- Design credit: kingseo., Spain. Asian restaurant, not Spanish cuisine.

**Observed:** a bold turquoise illustrated circular seal and bilingual wordmark on a pale hand-drawn mountain landscape. A restrained palette can still have character when the illustration and brand mark are specific.

**Useful for Aioli:** its real garlic/olive mark can be given a stronger role and tied to one deliberate accent. The identity can be present without inventing a new decorative symbol.

**Do not copy:** Asian landscape/lettering, turquoise seal or splash-screen composition. This is a secondary identity reference, not a menu-functionality reference.

## Research limits and selection

The earlier directly relevant Spanish restaurant records—7 Portes and El Amigo—remain useful context, but this pass deliberately sought distinct compositional approaches rather than accumulating similar cream/serif sites. Attempts to discover additional Spanish/tapas award records did not yield primary evidence strong enough to claim new distinctions. The five stronger additional references above supply independent principles; Overseas is retained as a secondary study with its availability limitation explicit.

All six archived opening compositions were visually inspected in the browser; Völlerei's two secondary archive views and Locanda Margon's menu-entry archive were also inspected. Current live homepage/location and menu behavior were inspected for Burrito Madre and 1NUL8. No reference assets were copied into Aioli.
