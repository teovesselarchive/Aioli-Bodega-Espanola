# Aioli Bodega Española — actual interior research

Research: 5 November 2026. Correct restaurant: **1800 L Street, Sacramento**. This pass checked all 116 entries in Aioli's public WordPress media library, the restaurant's official links, its owner's restaurant-group page, and attributed local photography.

## Most useful recovered wide photograph: official courtyard

**[Full patio photo — official Aioli image](https://aiolibodega.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/aioli-atmo-01a.jpeg)** — 1024 × 681 pixels. WordPress upload record: 26 March 2021. Local source: `work/research/aioli-interiors/aioli-atmo-01a.jpeg`.

## Implication for the redesign

The evidence supports **warm terracotta, copper, muted plum, creamy paper, dark wood and leafy green**, with selective ceramic color. The dominant experience is a lively, handmade Mediterranean bodega and shaded garden. Use authentic patio/detail photography to supply texture and character; let paper-toned menu surfaces remain easy to read. Clay-cubby rhythm, delicate metal botanical forms or carefully limited ceramic border details can inform original composition.

Palette direction is an inference from the photos, not a claim of measured paint colors. The actual room contains several kinds of texture and color, so a single flat burgundy background will not convey its character.
